This guide is for HR admins. When a supervisee changes supervisors, for example a supervisor leaves or a caseload rebalances, reassign the supervisee so the new supervisor picks up their record without losing history.

## Reassign a supervisee

1. From the team page at `/dashboard/team` or the roster, choose the supervisee.
2. Reassign them to the new supervisor.

AuditHalo ends the current supervisor assignment and creates a new one. The supervisee's hours, sessions, and evidence stay intact; only the supervising relationship changes.

[Screenshot: the reassign control on the team or roster page showing the supervisor selection]

## History is preserved

Reassignment does not erase the prior relationship. AuditHalo records:

- The end of the previous assignment.
- The start of the new assignment, marked as transferred from the previous supervisor.

This transfer chain is part of the audit trail, so a reviewer can see who supervised a clinician and when. See [Reading the audit log](/docs/audit-log/reading-the-audit-log).

> **Note:** Sessions already signed by the previous supervisor remain valid and sealed. Reassignment changes who supervises going forward; it does not alter past evidence.

## What's next

- Work the roster after a change. See [Managing your roster](/docs/team/managing-your-roster).
- Confirm the change in the audit log. See [Reading the audit log](/docs/audit-log/reading-the-audit-log).
