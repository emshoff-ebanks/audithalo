This guide covers inviting people into your organization. Who you can invite depends on your role, and one invitation type requires a two-factor code.

## Who can invite whom

- **Supervisors** can invite supervisees to their own caseload.
- **HR admins** can invite any role: supervisees, supervisors, HR admins, and executives.

Both roles work from the same invitation model: AuditHalo emails a secure link, the recipient accepts and sets a password, and they join the organization.

## Invite a supervisee

A supervisor invites from the roster; an HR admin can invite from the team page.

1. Go to `/dashboard/roster` (supervisor) or `/dashboard/team` (HR admin).
2. Select **Invite a supervisee**.
3. Enter their email, and their name if you have it.
4. Optionally assign the state rule and obligation start date now, so tracking begins on acceptance.
5. Send the invitation.

## Invite staff roles (HR admin)

From the team page at `/dashboard/team`, an HR admin can invite the other roles.

1. Go to `/dashboard/team`.
2. Select **Invite Supervisor**, **Invite HR Admin**, or **Invite Executive**.
3. Enter the person's email and name.
4. Send the invitation.

[Screenshot: the team page showing the Invite Supervisor, Invite HR Admin, and Invite Executive actions]

> **Warning:** Inviting an HR admin requires a two-factor code, since HR admins have org-wide write access. Set up 2FA first. See [Securing your account with two-factor authentication](/docs/account/two-factor-authentication).

> **Note:** Executive seats are limited per organization. Executives are read-only oversight accounts; they cannot change anything or see PHI.

## What happens on acceptance

When someone accepts, AuditHalo creates their account and membership in one step. If you attached a rule and supervisor to a supervisee invitation, those are applied on acceptance. The inviter is notified.

Invitations can be resent (new link, extended expiry) or canceled from the roster or team page.

## Inviting many people at once

For a large onboarding, import from a CSV instead of inviting individually. See [Bulk CSV import](/docs/team/bulk-csv-import).
