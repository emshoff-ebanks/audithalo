This guide is for HR admins onboarding many people at once. Instead of inviting one person at a time, import a CSV from any HRIS or spreadsheet, review what AuditHalo parsed, and send the invitations in a single batch.

## Import a CSV

1. Go to the team page at `/dashboard/team` and open the import at `/dashboard/team/import`.
2. Paste your CSV or upload the file.
3. Review the parsed preview.
4. Send the batch of invitations.

[Screenshot: the CSV import page showing the parsed preview with per-row status]

## Read the preview

Before anything is sent, AuditHalo parses your rows and shows a status for each:

- **Add:** a valid new person who will be invited.
- **Skip:** someone who is already a member or already has an open invitation.
- **Error:** a row that could not be parsed or is missing required data.

Fix any error rows in your source and re-import, or proceed to send the valid ones. AuditHalo does not send duplicate invitations to people it skips.

> **Note:** Importing sends invitations; it does not create active accounts. Each person still accepts their own invitation and sets their own password, the same as an individual invite. See [Inviting your team](/docs/team/inviting-team-members).

## After the import

Invited people appear as pending on the roster until they accept. Resend or cancel a pending invitation from the roster as needed. See [Managing your roster](/docs/team/managing-your-roster).
