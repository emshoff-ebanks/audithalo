This guide walks through the core loop that produces audit-ready proof: log a supervision session, sign it, and collect the countersignature that seals it. When every required signer has signed, AuditHalo generates an evidence package with a tamper-evident hash.

The order is fixed. The supervisor signs first, then the supervisee countersigns. This is enforced in the product, not just a convention.

## Log the session

The supervisor records the session. You can log a session you held ad hoc, or record one you scheduled in advance.

1. Open the supervisee's detail page at `/dashboard/roster/[superviseeId]`.
2. Select **Log session**.
3. Choose the supervision type: individual, triadic, or group.
4. Enter the date and duration.
5. For a group session, add the other attendees.
6. Save the session.

The session appears in the session log marked as awaiting signature.

[Screenshot: the Log session form showing the supervision type and duration fields]

> **Tip:** If you scheduled the session with a Teams or Google Meet link, you can generate an AI-assisted note from the transcript before signing. See [AI-assisted session notes](/docs/sessions/ai-session-notes).

## Sign as the supervisor

1. Open the session to reach the sign page at `/sign/[sessionId]`.
2. Review the details: date, duration, type, and your credentials.
3. Select **Sign session**.
4. Select the checkbox: *I confirm that I intend to electronically sign this session record.*
5. Submit your signature.

AuditHalo records your signature with your name, role, timestamp, and IP address. The session now waits for the supervisee.

[Screenshot: the sign page with the supervisor signature confirmation checkbox]

> **Warning:** The supervisee cannot countersign until you have signed. Sign promptly so their part is not blocked.

## Countersign as the supervisee

1. Open the session from your notifications or the session log.
2. Review the same session details.
3. Select **Sign session**.
4. Confirm intent with the checkbox.
5. Submit your signature.

## The evidence package seals automatically

When every required signer has signed, AuditHalo:

- Sets the signed timestamp.
- Builds a canonical record of the session and its signatures.
- Generates a SHA-256 hash of that record so any later change is detectable.
- Notifies both signers that the evidence package is sealed.

For a group session, every attendee must sign before the package seals.

[Screenshot: a sealed session showing the evidence package and its verification hash]

> **Note:** A sealed package is downloadable as a PDF and independently verifiable. See [Understanding evidence packages](/docs/evidence/understanding-evidence-packages).
