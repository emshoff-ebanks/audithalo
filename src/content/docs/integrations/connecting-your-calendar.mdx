This guide shows you how to connect a calendar so AuditHalo can schedule supervision from inside the app: create the meeting, add the video link, and write the event to your calendar. Connecting also enables pulling a meeting transcript later for AI-assisted notes.

You connect your own calendar from your account page. Each person connects their own; connecting is per user, not per organization.

## Providers you can connect

- **Microsoft (Teams + Outlook).** Creates Teams meetings and writes to Outlook Calendar.
- **Google (Meet + Calendar).** Creates Google Meet meetings and writes to Google Calendar.

## Connect a calendar

1. Go to your account page at `/dashboard/account`.
2. Find the **Calendar integrations** card.
3. Next to the provider you use, select **Connect**.
4. Sign in with that provider and grant AuditHalo access when prompted.

After you authorize, the provider shows as connected, along with the account you connected as.

[Screenshot: the Calendar integrations card showing Microsoft and Google with a Connect action]

> **Note:** AuditHalo stores your calendar authorization encrypted and uses it only to create and read the supervision events you schedule.

## Set a preferred provider

If you connect both providers, set one as the default for new sessions.

1. In the **Calendar integrations** card, find the connected provider you want as default.
2. Select **Set as preferred**.

New sessions you schedule use the preferred provider unless you choose otherwise.

## Disconnect a calendar

1. In the **Calendar integrations** card, find the connected provider.
2. Select **Disconnect** and confirm.

You can reconnect at any time. Disconnecting stops AuditHalo from creating or reading events with that provider; it does not delete events already on your calendar.

> **Warning:** If you disconnect the calendar used for upcoming scheduled sessions, AuditHalo can no longer update or cancel those events on your calendar. Reconnect, or manage those events manually, before disconnecting mid-series.
