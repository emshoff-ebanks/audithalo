This guide covers the calendar at `/dashboard/calendar`: how to move between views, what each session status means, and how reminders work. The calendar is where you see supervision at a glance and jump into a meeting.

What you see is scoped to your role: a supervisor sees their assigned supervisees' sessions, an HR admin sees the whole organization, and a supervisee or executive sees their own sessions.

## Switch views

The calendar offers three views:

- **Week** for the working week at a glance.
- **Month** for the broader schedule.
- **List** for a linear agenda you can scan top to bottom.

[Screenshot: the calendar in week view showing scheduled supervision sessions]

## Read session status

Each event carries a status:

- **Scheduled:** booked and upcoming.
- **Completed:** the session time has passed.
- **Canceled:** called off; removed from the connected calendar.
- **No-show:** marked as not held.
- **Signed:** recorded and fully signed into an evidence package.

## Join a meeting

For sessions scheduled with Teams or Google Meet, the event shows a join link. Select it to open the meeting in your provider at session time.

## Reminders

AuditHalo sends reminders so sessions and signatures do not slip:

- A reminder 1 hour before a scheduled session.
- A reminder 15 minutes before.
- A sign reminder to the supervisor if a session's end time passes without a signature.

> **Note:** Reminders and calendar events depend on a connected calendar for video links. To connect or change providers, see [Connecting your calendar](/docs/integrations/connecting-your-calendar).
