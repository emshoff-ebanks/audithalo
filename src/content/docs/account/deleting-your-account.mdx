This guide explains what happens when you delete your AuditHalo account: the grace period, the immediate sign-out, and how it interacts with your organization's records. Deletion is reversible for a window, then permanent, so read this before you start.

## Delete your account

1. Go to your account page at `/dashboard/account`.
2. Open the delete-account section.
3. Confirm with your password.

You are signed out immediately and your account enters a 30-day grace period.

[Screenshot: the delete-account section on the account page]

## The 30-day grace period

Deletion is a soft delete. For 30 days your account is deactivated but recoverable; after that, it is purged. During the grace period you cannot sign in, and your sessions are invalidated.

> **Warning:** After the 30-day grace period, deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you are unsure, sign out of all devices instead and decide later. See [Managing your password and email](/docs/account/password-and-email).

## What happens to organization records

Deleting your personal account does not erase the organization's evidence. Sessions you signed remain sealed and valid, because an evidence package is a historical record that must hold up to an audit regardless of later account changes. Your name and signature on past sessions are preserved.

## Billing

If you are a supervisor or HR admin with a subscription, manage or cancel billing separately through the billing portal before or after deleting. Deleting your account does not automatically end a subscription. See [Managing your subscription](/docs/billing/managing-your-subscription).

## What's next

- Consider signing out everywhere instead. See [Managing your password and email](/docs/account/password-and-email).
- Handle your subscription. See [Managing your subscription](/docs/billing/managing-your-subscription).
