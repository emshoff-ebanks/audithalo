This guide explains AuditHalo's three plans and what each includes. The supervisor is the buyer; supervisees are always free, so you pay for the people who carry board liability, not the people accumulating hours.

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. You can compare plans and start a trial from `/dashboard/billing`.

## The three plans

### Solo Supervisor

For an individual supervisor with a small caseload.

- 89 dollars per month, or 890 dollars per year.
- Up to 3 supervisees.
- 10 AI-assisted transcripts per month.

### Practice

For a group practice with multiple supervisors and an HR admin.

- 49 dollars per month base, plus 25 dollars per supervisee per month.
- Unlimited supervisees; you buy seats as you grow.
- 100 AI-assisted transcripts per month for the organization.
- Bulk CSV import and a 7-year audit-log retention default.

### Enterprise

For larger organizations with procurement and compliance requirements.

- Custom pricing, managed by contract rather than self-serve checkout.
- SOC 2, a signed BAA, and a dedicated customer success manager.

See [Enterprise plans](/docs/billing/enterprise).

## Supervisees are free

You never pay per supervisee for access. On Solo, supervisees are included up to the caseload limit. On Practice, a seat is what you buy as your roster grows. Either way, the supervisee's account, dashboard, and hour logging cost them nothing.

## The free trial

- Every plan begins with a 14-day free trial.
- You can invite supervisees and sign sessions during the trial.
- Stripe sends a reminder as the trial nears its end.

> **Note:** AI-assisted transcript limits are per billing period. Reaching the limit pauses AI note generation until the next period or a plan change; it does not affect logging or signing.
