This guide explains the gaps AuditHalo shows on a supervisee's detail page. A gap is a specific, actionable reason a supervisee is not fully compliant. Each one carries a severity and a next step, so a yellow or red status always tells you exactly what to do.

## Severity, quickly

- **Info:** context, no action forced.
- **Warning:** attention needed; contributes to yellow status.
- **Blocker:** serious; drives red status until resolved.

Gaps are grouped by type on the detail page so related items appear together.

## The five gap actions

Each gap is one of five kinds, and the kind tells you the move.

### 1. Attestation

A fact needs to be recorded, for example a supervision contract filed with the board or a required training completed. Clear it by recording the attestation with its date. See [Assigning and attesting rules](/docs/rules/assigning-and-attesting-rules).

### 2. Recurring behavior

An ongoing cadence is behind, for example an individual supervision session is due. Clear it by doing and logging the next session.

### 3. Data correction

Existing data is wrong or inconsistent, and the gap highlights the specific session rows involved. Clear it by correcting the offending entries.

### 4. Data accumulation

Nothing is wrong; the supervisee simply needs more hours. The gap shows progress toward the target. Clear it by continuing to accumulate and approve hours.

### 5. Time warning

A deadline is approaching or has passed, for example a permit expiration window. The gap shows days remaining or overdue status. Clear it by acting before the date.

## Work by exception

Use the roster's at-risk filter to find red supervisees, open each one, and clear blockers first. A blocker is what holds a supervisee at red; resolving it is usually the fastest way back toward green. See [Understanding your compliance dashboard](/docs/rules/understanding-compliance-status).

> **Note:** A supervisee on approved leave has cadence checks paused, so a recurring-behavior gap will not appear for the leave period.

## What's next

- Record the facts a rule needs. See [Assigning and attesting rules](/docs/rules/assigning-and-attesting-rules).
- Keep approved hours current. See [Approving and rejecting practice hours](/docs/practice-hours/approving-practice-hours).
