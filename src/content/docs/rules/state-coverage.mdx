This guide lists the states AuditHalo covers with board-verified canonical rules and explains what to do if your state is not among them. Each state page on the marketing site carries the license details and the board citation; this article is the quick index.

## Covered states and licenses

AuditHalo covers ten states at launch, each mapped to its pre-licensure credential:

| State | License | Credential name |
|---|---|---|
| Arizona | LAC | Licensed Associate Counselor |
| California | APCC | Associate Professional Clinical Counselor |
| Delaware | LACMH | Licensed Associate Counselor of Mental Health |
| Florida | RMHCI | Registered Mental Health Counselor Intern |
| Louisiana | PLPC | Provisional Licensed Professional Counselor |
| North Carolina | LCMHCA | Licensed Clinical Mental Health Counselor Associate |
| New York | LMHC-LP | Licensed Mental Health Counselor, Limited Permit |
| Ohio | LPC | Licensed Professional Counselor (toward LPCC) |
| Texas | LPC-Associate | Licensed Professional Counselor Associate |
| Washington | LMHCA | Licensed Mental Health Counselor Associate |

For the full requirements and the board citation for a state, see its page under [audithalo.com/states](https://audithalo.com/states).

> **Note:** Each canonical rule is versioned and grounded in the state's administrative code. When a board changes a requirement, AuditHalo publishes a new rule version and offers to upgrade affected assignments. See [Rule versions and drift](/docs/rules/rule-versions-and-drift).

## If your state is not listed

A supervisee must be on a rule to be tracked. If AuditHalo does not yet cover your state, an HR admin on a Practice or Enterprise plan can author a custom rule from the state's administrative code.

See [Creating custom rules](/docs/rules/creating-custom-rules).
