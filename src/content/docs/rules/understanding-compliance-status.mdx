This guide explains how to read compliance status in AuditHalo. Every supervisee has a status that summarizes how their logged, approved hours measure against their assigned state rule, so you can tell at a glance who is on track and who needs attention.

The same status appears in three places: the roster, the supervisee detail page, and, for supervisees, their own dashboard. Supervisors and supervisees see the same numbers.

## The three risk levels

AuditHalo reduces each supervisee's standing to one of three colors.

- **Green: on track.** No warnings or blockers. Hours and cadence are keeping pace with the rule.
- **Yellow: needs attention.** One or more warnings. Something is behind or approaching a deadline, but nothing is disqualifying yet.
- **Red: at risk.** At least one blocker. A requirement is failing in a way that threatens the supervised hours if it is not resolved.

## Progress bars and totals

On the supervisee detail page, progress bars show accumulation toward the rule's targets:

- Practice hours toward the required total.
- Supervision hours toward the required total.

Only approved practice hours count toward these bars, so a supervisee's total can lag until a supervisor approves pending entries. See [Approving and rejecting practice hours](/docs/practice-hours/approving-practice-hours).

[Screenshot: the supervisee detail page showing practice and supervision progress bars]

## Reading the roster at scale

The roster at `/dashboard/roster` shows every supervisee with their status badge, practice-hour progress, and pending-signature count. Use the filters to work by exception:

- Filter to at-risk to see only red supervisees.
- Filter to pending signatures to find sessions waiting to be signed.
- Search by name to jump to one person.

HR admins see the whole organization and can also filter by supervisor. Supervisors see their own caseload.

> **Note:** A supervisee on leave has cadence checks paused, so an approved leave shows as a leave badge rather than dragging their status to red.

## From status to action

A yellow or red status is backed by specific gaps, each with a next step, on the supervisee detail page. Status tells you who needs attention; the gaps tell you exactly what to do.
