This guide is for the executive role: board members, external auditors, and partner-clinic leaders who need oversight without touching the day-to-day work. Your view is read-only by design.

When you sign in, AuditHalo takes you straight to the executive dashboard at `/dashboard/executive`.

## What you can and cannot do

You can:

- View the executive dashboard: an organization-wide compliance rollup.
- View the audit log.
- Export the audit log (no two-factor step required for executives).
- View the calendar for your own sessions.

You cannot:

- Sign sessions, log sessions, or generate notes.
- Invite anyone or manage the team.
- Change any setting or access billing.

No PHI or session-note content appears anywhere in your view. The executive role is built to give assurance without exposing clinical detail.

## Read the executive dashboard

The dashboard summarizes the whole organization in a set of cards:

- Total supervisees.
- Number who need attention.
- Pending signatures.
- Supervision hours this month.
- Sessions scheduled this week.
- No-shows in the last 30 days.
- Sealed evidence packages this month.

Below the cards, a "Needs attention" table lists the most at-risk supervisees with their primary supervisor, credential, and status, and a breakdown of pending signatures by supervisor.

[Screenshot: the executive dashboard at /dashboard/executive showing the summary cards and Needs attention table]

> **Note:** Compliance status reads as green, yellow, or red. For what each color means, see [Understanding your compliance dashboard](/docs/rules/understanding-compliance-status).

## Review and export the audit log

The audit log is your evidence that the organization's supervision is being tracked and signed.

1. Go to `/dashboard/audit-log`.
2. Filter by action type to focus on what a reviewer asked about.
3. Export to CSV or JSON when you need a record to share.

As an executive you can export without a two-factor step.
